# Code of conduct

Whetstone is a place to learn and to help others prepare. Everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, reviews and submitted questions) is expected to:

- **Be respectful.** Critique ideas and code, never people. No harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind.
- **Be generous.** Assume good intent, explain your reasoning, and remember that many contributors are learning.
- **Respect confidentiality.** Don't share anyone's private information, or confidential details from employers or interviews.
- **Keep it on topic.** Discussions should help the project and its learners.

The owner may edit or remove contributions, and block participants, that don't follow these expectations. To report a problem privately, contact the owner through their GitHub profile ([@anand1296](https://github.com/anand1296)).
